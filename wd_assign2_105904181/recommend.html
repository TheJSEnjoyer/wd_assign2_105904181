In this page, the users will do a small survey with 3 preferences that will give them a recommendation based on the customer's preferences to make sure they eat as the way they want while making sure they are enjoying the meal!

How does this part work?

The survey comes with 3 options: dietary preference, budget range, and the reason for dining. After the customer has answered, the website will look for the recommendation based on the choices the customer gave and provide the best match for them.
